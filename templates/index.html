The DineMind Experience

Unlike black-box algorithms or sponsored placements, DineMind computes mathematical similarity vectors across verified restaurant features to ensure every recommendation is explainable and reproducible.

40%
Cuisine Match

0.75 × Coverage + 0.25 × Jaccard overlap between your chosen flavours and the restaurant's menu offerings.

20%
Location Proximity

Exact locality match awarded maximum weight (1.0), with city-wide fallback alignment (0.6).

20%
Budget Matching

Evaluates tier proximity (1 to 4) and proportional distance to your maximum numeric budget ceiling.

15%
Rating Quality

Bayesian vote-damped normalization: 0.85 × r_norm + 0.15 × log1p(votes)/log1p(1000) prevents low-vote outlier distortion.
