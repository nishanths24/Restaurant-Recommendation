About DineMind & Methodology

Engineering a transparent, preference-first restaurant recommendation engine using content-based filtering.

Mission & Core Philosophy

DineMind was developed as part of the Cognifyz Technologies Machine Learning Internship Program (Task 2: Restaurant Recommendation System). The primary objective is to recommend restaurants according to a user's stated dining criteria — rather than relying solely on item-to-item similarity or user-to-user history.

By evaluating stated user preferences against multi-attribute restaurant vectors (cuisine overlap, location match, budget fit, and Bayesian vote-damped ratings), DineMind guarantees explainable, reproducible recommendations without cold-start failure.

Preprocessing Highlights:
  • BOM handling: Sanitized \ufeffRestaurant ID and mapped canonical columns in config.py.
  • Encoding normalization: Corrected mojibake in city names (Bras_lia → Brasilia, So Paulo → Sao Paulo).
  • Cuisine parsing: Tokenized comma-separated strings into 146 unique standardized cuisine tokens.
  • Rating handling: Preserved 2,148 unrated listings (0.0 rating) with transparent neutral scoring, preventing false imputation.

Recommendation Scoring Formulation

Stage A: Hard Constraints (Pruning)

When strict filtering is enabled by the user, candidates violating location, budget ceiling, or minimum rating are excluded prior to scoring.

Stage B: Multi-Criteria Relevance Ranking
Score = 100 * (∑ w_i * s_i) / (∑ w_i)
Preference Component Weight Methodology
Cuisine Match 40% 0.75 × Coverage + 0.25 × Jaccard Overlap between user preferences and restaurant offerings.
Location Fit 20% Exact locality match = 1.0; same city match = 0.6; non-matching = 0.0.
Budget Fit 20% Closeness to price tier (1 to 4) or proportional closeness to maximum numeric budget.
Rating Quality 15% Star rating normalized to [0, 1] with logarithmic vote count damping: 0.85 × r_norm + 0.15 × log1p(votes)/log1p(1000).
Service Features 5% Verification of online delivery and table booking availability when explicitly requested.
Active Renormalization: If a user leaves a preference blank (e.g. leaves budget unspecified), that weight is excluded from both numerator and denominator, preventing unfair penalties on unselected criteria.

Ethical AI & Data Integrity Commitments

  • Zero Data Fabrication: No artificial restaurant names, fake ratings, or hallucinated reviews.
  • No Dietary Guesswork: Because the Cognifyz dataset does not include dietary tags (halal, vegan, allergen-free), DineMind explicitly disables dietary filters rather than guessing from names or cuisine labels.
  • Verifiable Explanations: Every match tag ("Within budget", "Matches cuisine") is directly derived from verified record values.
  • Deterministic Ranking: Tie-breaking is performed deterministically (Score → Rating → Votes → Name), ensuring reproducible results.
